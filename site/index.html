What’s inside

Every part of it can be changed

Aqua Browser is Mozilla’s Firefox engine wearing Aqua MD’s glass. The look, the home page and the profiles are all yours to set.

Profiles

Home and Work, kept apart

Each profile has its own cookies, history, passwords and home page. Switch with the chip next to the address bar, and each one opens in its own window.

Home page

A home page you build

Sixteen kinds of widget: a dot-matrix clock, weather, search, shortcuts, notes, to-do lists, a calendar, a focus timer, countdowns, news feeds and your own pictures. Drag them around and pick each one’s size.

Vault

Passwords and cards stay with you

Aqua Browser saves and fills in your passwords and payment cards itself, with no Google account. Lock them behind a master password.

Colour

Seven colours, plus Auto

Blue, teal, green, orange, red, pink and purple, the same seven as Aqua MD. Auto fades through all of them on a timer you choose, from ten seconds to an hour.

Look

As much glass as you like

Set the glass, the blur and how round the corners are. Put the tabs along the top or down the side, show the address in dot-matrix or plain type, or add your own CSS.

Ad block

Ads blocked from the start

uBlock Origin is set up the first time you open Aqua Browser, so pages load without ads and trackers.

Engine

Mozilla’s engine underneath

Aqua Browser runs on Firefox ESR, Mozilla’s extended-support release, so websites work the way they do in Firefox.

Import

Bring your stuff over

Import bookmarks, passwords and history from Chrome, Edge or another browser the first time you open it.

Aqua Browser doesn’t update itself yet. New versions appear here and on GitHub Releases; installing one over the old keeps your profiles, passwords and bookmarks.

From the maker of Aqua MD

The look started with a MiniDisc deck

Aqua MD is a see-through MiniDisc deck for your desktop. It plays Spotify, your own music and the sound from your videos, with lyrics, a clock and widgets on a dot-matrix display. Aqua Browser borrows its glass, its display and its seven colours.
